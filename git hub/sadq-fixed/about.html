Who We Are

Our Story

The Students Association of Darul Quran (SADQ) is the official student body of Darul Quran Islamic Academy, Pazhur. We are a community of students dedicated to the memorization, understanding, and living of the Quran, while embracing modern tools and technology to serve our community better.

Our members are disciples of Rahmathullah Al-Qasimi, and our work spans education, creative expression, community service, and digital innovation. This platform is our digital home — a place to share knowledge, offer services, publish magazines, showcase projects, and grow together.
